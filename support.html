Support支持

How can we help?我们能为你做什么？

AIGlassAware requires iOS 16 or later and Bluetooth permission for nearby-device scanning.AIGlassAware 需要 iOS 16 或更高版本，并需要蓝牙权限才能扫描附近设备。

Open a support request提交支持请求 ↗
01

Scanning does not start扫描无法启动

  1. Open iOS Settings and allow Bluetooth access for AIGlassAware.打开 iOS 设置，允许 AIGlassAware 使用蓝牙。
  2. Confirm that Bluetooth is enabled.确认蓝牙已开启。
  3. Keep the app in the foreground while scanning.扫描时保持 App 在前台运行。
  4. Restore your purchase from Settings if scanning access is locked.如果扫描权限被锁定，请在设置页恢复购买。
02

Detection accuracy检测准确性

Bluetooth signal strength changes with walls, people, radio interference, and device power. Distance is approximate. A match does not indicate that a device is recording.墙体、人群、无线干扰与设备功率都会影响蓝牙信号强度。距离只是大致估算，匹配结果也不代表设备正在录制。

03

Purchase support购买支持

Use Restore Purchases in the app when using the same Apple Account. Billing, refunds, and payment-method issues are handled by Apple Support.使用同一 Apple 账户时，请在 App 内使用恢复购买。账单、退款与付款方式问题由 Apple 支持处理。

04

Before contacting us联系前请准备

Include the app version, iOS version, device model, and a short description of the issue. Do not include sensitive personal information.请附上 App 版本、iOS 版本、设备型号与简短的问题描述。请勿提供敏感个人信息。
