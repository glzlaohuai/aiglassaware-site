AIGlassAware Support

AIGlassAware requires iOS 16 or later and Bluetooth permission for nearby-device scanning.

Scanning does not start

  • Open iOS Settings and allow Bluetooth access for AIGlassAware.
  • Confirm that Bluetooth is enabled.
  • Keep the app in the foreground while scanning.
  • Restore your purchase from the Settings tab if scanning access is locked.

Detection accuracy

Bluetooth signal strength changes with walls, people, radio interference, and device power. Distance is approximate. A match does not indicate that a device is recording.

Purchase support
