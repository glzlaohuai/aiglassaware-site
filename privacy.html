Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

AIGlassAware is designed to provide nearby smart-glasses awareness while minimizing data collection.

Data we collect

AIGlassAware does not collect personal data, create user accounts, use advertising identifiers, or track users across apps or websites.

Bluetooth scanning

The app reads Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements visible to your device. It uses broadcast names, service identifiers, manufacturer data, and signal strength to estimate whether supported smart glasses may be nearby. The app does not connect to, control, block, or interfere with those devices.

On-device history

Detection history and app preferences are stored locally on your device. You can clear detection history in the app. Removing the app also removes its local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Rule updates

The app may download a device-fingerprint rules file from our content delivery endpoint. We do not use that request to build user profiles or for advertising.

Optional sample export

If you explicitly choose to export an unknown-device sample, iOS presents its standard share sheet. The sample can include a Bluetooth broadcast name, service identifiers, manufacturer data, signal strength, capture time, and rules version. It does not include the device system identifier, your location, contacts, or Apple Account. AIGlassAware does not automatically upload or receive the sample.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We receive entitlement status from Apple but do not receive your complete payment-card information.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method on the AIGlassAware support page.
