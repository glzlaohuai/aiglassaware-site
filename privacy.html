Privacy Policy隐私政策

Effective October 2, 2026生效日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日

The short version简要说明

AIGlassAware is designed to provide nearby smart-glasses awareness while minimizing data collection. Detection history stays on your device.AIGlassAware 旨在尽量减少数据收集的同时，帮助你感知附近可能存在的智能眼镜。检测历史保存在你的设备上。

Data we collect我们收集的数据

AIGlassAware does not collect personal data, create user accounts, use advertising identifiers, or track users across apps or websites.AIGlassAware 不收集个人数据、不创建用户账号、不使用广告标识符，也不会跨 App 或网站跟踪用户。

Bluetooth scanning蓝牙扫描

The app reads Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements visible to your device. It uses broadcast names, service identifiers, manufacturer data, and signal strength to estimate whether supported smart glasses may be nearby. The app does not connect to, control, block, or interfere with those devices.App 会读取设备可见的低功耗蓝牙广播，并使用广播名称、服务标识、厂商数据与信号强度，估算附近是否可能存在已支持的智能眼镜。App 不会连接、控制、阻断或干扰这些设备。

On-device history设备内历史记录

Detection history and app preferences are stored locally on your device. You can clear detection history in the app. Removing the app also removes its local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior controlled by Apple and your device settings.检测历史与 App 偏好设置保存在你的设备本地。你可以在 App 内清除检测历史。删除 App 也会删除其本地数据，但仍受 Apple 与设备设置所控制的常规 iOS 备份机制影响。

Rule updates规则更新

The app may download a device-fingerprint rules file from our content delivery endpoint. We do not use that request to build user profiles or for advertising.App 可能从我们的内容分发端点下载设备指纹规则文件。我们不会利用该请求建立用户画像或投放广告。

Optional sample export可选样本导出

If you explicitly choose to export an unknown-device sample, iOS presents its standard share sheet. The sample can include a Bluetooth broadcast name, service identifiers, manufacturer data, signal strength, capture time, and rules version. It does not include the device system identifier, your location, contacts, or Apple Account. AIGlassAware does not automatically upload or receive the sample.如果你主动选择导出未知设备样本，iOS 会显示系统分享面板。样本可能包含蓝牙广播名称、服务标识、厂商数据、信号强度、捕获时间与规则版本；不包含设备系统标识、位置、联系人或 Apple 账户。AIGlassAware 不会自动上传或接收该样本。

In-app purchasesApp 内购买

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We receive entitlement status from Apple but do not receive your complete payment-card information.购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。我们会从 Apple 获得权益状态，但不会获得完整的支付卡信息。

Children儿童

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.本 App 不面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人数据。

Changes政策变更

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.我们可能随 App 变化更新本政策。上方生效日期用于标识当前版本。

Contact联系我们

For privacy questions, use the contact method on the AIGlassAware support page.如有隐私问题，请通过 AIGlassAware 支持页面中的方式联系我们。
