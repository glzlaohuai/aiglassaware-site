AIGlassAware

Understand when smart glasses may be nearby using Bluetooth broadcast patterns and on-device device fingerprints.

Privacy by design

  • No account required.
  • No location permission requested.
  • Detection history stays on your device.
  • No connection to or control of nearby devices.

Detection is a signal-based estimate. It does not indicate that a device is recording and should not be used to accuse anyone.
