How it works工作方式

Three steps, all on your iPhone.三个步骤，全部在你的 iPhone 上完成。

  1. 1

    Listen监听

    With Bluetooth permission, the app reads advertisements visible while it is active in the foreground.获得蓝牙权限后，App 在前台运行时读取周围可见的广播。

  2. 2

    Compare比对

    Broadcast names, services, and manufacturer data are compared with on-device fingerprint rules.广播名称、服务标识与厂商数据会和设备内的指纹规则进行比对。

  3. 3

    Estimate估算

    Signal strength provides a rough proximity estimate and can trigger an alert for a suspected match.信号强度用于粗略估算距离，并在疑似匹配时触发提醒。

Important limitation重要使用边界

Bluetooth matches cannot confirm recording or a camera-free space.蓝牙匹配无法确认录制行为，也无法确认场所内没有摄像头。

This app cannot detect all hidden cameras or all smart glasses. It can only compare visible broadcasts with supported patterns. Devices may be missed or misidentified. Signal strength varies with the environment. Results should never be used to accuse anyone.本应用无法发现所有隐藏摄像头或所有智能眼镜，只能将可见广播与已支持特征进行比较，可能漏检或误判。信号强度受环境影响。请勿依据匹配结果指责任何人。

FAQ常见问题

What the app can and cannot tell you.了解 App 能告诉你什么，以及不能告诉你什么。

Can AIGlassAware find all hidden cameras or tell whether a device is recording?AIGlassAware 能找到所有隐藏摄像头，或判断设备是否正在录制吗？

No. It only matches some visible Bluetooth broadcasts. Devices that do not broadcast, use unsupported patterns, or are out of range may be missed. It cannot verify whether a device has a camera or is recording. No matches does not mean no cameras are present.不能。它只能匹配部分可见的蓝牙广播。不广播、特征未被支持或超出范围的设备可能被漏检。它无法确认设备是否配备摄像头或正在录制，没有匹配结果也不代表没有摄像头。

Does the app connect to smart glasses?App 会连接智能眼镜吗？

No. It passively reads visible Bluetooth advertisements and does not pair with, control, block, or interfere with nearby devices.不会。它只被动读取可见的蓝牙广播，不会配对、控制、阻断或干扰附近设备。

Why can results be inaccurate?为什么结果可能不准确？

Bluetooth patterns can overlap across products, some devices do not broadcast visible signals, and signal strength changes with the environment. Both scanning modes use the same matching rules and may produce false matches or miss devices.不同产品的蓝牙特征可能重叠，部分设备不发送可见广播，信号强度也受环境影响。两种扫描模式使用相同的匹配规则，都可能误报或漏检。

Where is my history stored?检测历史保存在哪里？

Detection history and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone and can be cleared in the app.检测历史与偏好设置保存在你的 iPhone 本地，并可在 App 内清除。
